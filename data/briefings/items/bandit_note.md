A small folded scrap of paper. It was on Drust's person. The hand
that wrote it is steady and unhurried.

  Three days more. Cargo stays in the cage. The gold-eyed one
  pays on delivery, not before. No fires after dark.

There is no name signed and no seal stamped. The paper is good paper,
not common to the road; it has not travelled long before reaching
Drust. The phrase "the gold-eyed one" appears nowhere in Vael's
common idiom.
