A coin smaller than a Vael half-crown, struck in a metal that does
not match copper, silver, or anything Mara has seen pass through her
forge. The face shows a circle within a circle and a mark like a
narrow eye. The edge is milled in an unfamiliar pattern.

Mara has a sketch of one already pinned beside her bellows. She made
it the last time a traveler dropped one on her counter, three months
ago. She has not been able to match the mint or the mark to anything
she knows.

It is not currency Vael honors. It is not, by appearance, a forgery
of anything Vael honors. It is something else.
