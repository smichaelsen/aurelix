A small flat piece of bone, smoothed and pierced and tied with cord
to the cage that held the hatchling. The name carved on it, in old
hand, is one word:

  Iskar.

The carving is careful. The strokes are not the work of a bandit
killing time at a fire. Whoever made this tag named the creature
deliberately, and did so before the cage came into the bandits'
hands.
