A small charm of bone and waxed cord, the bone shaped to a long oval
and marked with a single sigil of the Order. It is the kind a priest
gives a young person before a journey, or keeps beside a prayer cushion
for the next person who will need one.

It was tucked into the seam of the prayer cushion at the front of the
chapel, where a careful kneel would find it and an inattentive one
would not. Sister Edda does not appear to be looking for it.

Worn close, the charm steadies the wearer's step. Those who carry one
move a little faster on roads they are afraid of.
