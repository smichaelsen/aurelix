On the night of the new moon, between the last cart bell and the hour
the chapel candles go cold, smoke rose from the old tower into a sky
that should have been clear. Wings, heavier than any owl's, passed
over the forest path at low height. The smoke smelled of old burn.
Whatever flew did not call.

Orren saw the smoke from the road outside the tavern. He saw the wings
cross the moon. He has not slept right since. He has told no one,
sober or otherwise, what he saw plainly.
