Someone saw lights at the old tower one night. Maybe smoke. Maybe
the moon catching something. Different people tell it different
ways. Stories get bigger every time they are told, and shorter every
time a sober man tells them.

A traveler said something to Mara at the smithy; Mara did not press.
A child at the well says the dragon ate a sheep, but the well has
no sheep. The reeve has heard enough versions to post a notice.
