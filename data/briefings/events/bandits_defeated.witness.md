The camp set up between the two leaning pines and the fall of stones
has been cleared. Three of its men are dead by the fire. Drust, who
ran it, is dead at the threshold of his tent with a notched-guard
sword still in his hand. The cage near the fire is open. The cooking
pot is overturned. The forest path is open again to anyone who knows
where it leads.

A small folded note was on Drust's person. It mentioned the gold-eyed
one. A small foreign coin was in his belt-purse.
