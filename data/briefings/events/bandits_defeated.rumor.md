The bandits that were on the forest path north of the village are
not on the forest path anymore. Some say the reeve sent men. Some
say the stranger from the south did it. Some say they moved on
because they were paid to. The reeve has not posted a notice about
it, so it is not yet certain.

A traveler may again take the path if the traveler is willing.
