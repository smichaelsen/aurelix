The stranger from the south has returned from the forest with a
small creature at his heel. The creature is dark, lizard-like,
larger than a cat and smaller than a dog. It has wings it does not
use. It watches people without blinking. It does not run from the
stranger and the stranger does not chain it.

It is not a dog. It is not a hawk. It is not anything anyone in
Marlow has seen kept as company.

The villagers who have seen it walk together with the stranger now
know this much of it: it exists, it is here, and it has come from
the forest where the smoke has been coming from.
