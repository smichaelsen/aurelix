In the bandit camp off the forest path, a wicker-and-iron cage stood
near the cooking fire. Inside the cage was a hatchling drake, dark
as soot and small enough to carry. The cage had a bone tag tied to
it. The name on the tag was Iskar, carved by a careful hand.

After the bandits had fallen, the cage opened. The hatchling did not
run. He came forward and watched the stranger who had opened the
cage. After a long moment he chose to follow.

No one else saw this. The bandits were dead. The forest was quiet.
