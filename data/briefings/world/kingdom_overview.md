Vael is the kingdom. King Aldric the Quiet sits in Ostgate, the seat of
the Crown, four days' ride south of Marlow's Hollow along the King's
Road. He is not loved, but he is not feared the way his father was, and
that is taken as progress.

The kingdom keeps to three roads, two rivers, and the strip of farmland
between them. North of that strip the forest begins and grows for ten
days' walk before anyone has bothered to map it. The frontier villages,
Marlow among them, are where the kingdom thins.

There is a Crown, a tithe, and a court. There are reeves in every
village to keep the peace and collect what the Crown asks. There is the
Order, the priests, who serve the Light and who answer to their own
chapter in Ostgate. There are merchants who travel between the towns
and complain about the roads. There are the forest folk, who are not
the Crown's. And there are bandits in the gaps.

Most people in Vael will never see Ostgate. Most have never met a
priest above the rank of sister. Most have never seen a dragon. Of
that last group there are still some, in Marlow, who would now correct
the speaker.
