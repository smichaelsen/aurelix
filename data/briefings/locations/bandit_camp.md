A camp set up off the path between two leaning pines and a fall of
large stones, perhaps half a day's walk into the Forest Edge. Four
men hold it: three regulars and one named Drust who carries a sword
with a notched guard and gives the orders.

The camp is not a robbing camp. The men do not seem to want
travelers; when they hear footsteps they hide. They are paid to be
there. They keep one cage near the cooking fire. The cage has a
hatchling drake inside, kept quiet and kept fed. The cage has a bone
tag tied to it.

The men have been there for at least a week. They are waiting for a
delivery. Their notes mention "the gold-eyed one" who pays on arrival.

Drust does not know his employer's face. He knows the coin he was paid
in advance, and he has a small sample of it, which is unlike any
currency in Vael.
