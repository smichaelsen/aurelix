Marlow's Hollow is a frontier village in the northern reach of Vael.
About sixty households if you count the outliers. A square with a well
in the middle. A tavern, a smithy, a chapel, a reeve's house, and the
last useful road before the woods take over.

The village sits where the King's Road runs out and the forest path
takes over. North of Marlow is the Forest Edge; beyond that, the old
tower nobody likes to mention, and beyond that, the deep woods, which
might as well be another country.

Marlow has been quiet for as long as anyone who matters can remember.
Recently it has not been quiet. There were sightings near the tower.
There is a notice posted by Reeve Halden on the board in the square,
offering coin and bread to whoever brings him proof. Some say there
have been bandits closer than usual. The smith has had no order from a
proper traveler in two weeks. The priest's evening service is cancelled.

Strangers from the south are not common, and are received with the
politeness Marlow extends to people it is not yet sure about.
