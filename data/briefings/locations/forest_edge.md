The Forest Edge is the strip of woodland that begins where Marlow's
fields end and continues north until the rocks rise to the old tower.
The path through it is single-file, root-broken, and not maintained.
There are wolves. There are old shrines that nobody attends. There
are clearings that did not used to be there.

Hunters from Marlow rarely go past the second bend, and not at all
after dusk. The forest folk who live deeper sometimes appear on the
path, never with introductions, and pass through without speaking.

Recently there has been a bandit camp set up off the path, between
two leaning pines and a fall of large stones. Smoke can be smelled at
times. Travelers from the north have stopped coming through Marlow.

From the Forest Edge, on a clear day, the old tower is visible to the
north as a black mark against the sky.
