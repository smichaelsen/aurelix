The old tower stands a half day's walk north of Marlow's Hollow, on a
rise of bare stone above the tree line. It was already a ruin when
Marlow was founded. Nobody alive remembers what it was for, who built
it, or who broke it. The villagers do not go there. The forest folk
say it was once a watch and that something was watched.

The stones are dark and do not catch frost the way other stones do.
Children dare each other to walk to its base. Most do not. The few
who have come back say only that it was cold there, even in summer,
and that they did not stay.

In recent weeks there has been smoke. Some say light. Some say wings.
Reeve Halden has posted a bounty for proof of what is there.
