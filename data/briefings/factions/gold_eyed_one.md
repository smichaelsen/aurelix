A name that appears in writing in places it should not. It is used by
people who pay other people to do work in the forest north of Marlow,
and to keep their mouths shut about it.

Nobody who can be spoken to in Marlow knows the gold-eyed one by face,
by voice, or by title. The phrase is not Vael's. The coin used to pay
in their name is not Vael's either. Edda, if shown the phrase in
writing, would recognise old patterns in it.

There is no witness-tier briefing for this faction. There is, so far,
only the rumor of one.
