Kael was born in the southern fens, near where the rivers go slow and
the marshes hold their dead. His father drank himself out before Kael
could read, and his mother caught the fever when he was twelve. He
worked the gravedigger's trade for three years afterward.

He answered the reeve's notice because anywhere is a better place to
be than another summer of digging. He owns a rusty sword that does not
have a name, a traveler's cloak that was his mother's, and very little
else to lose.

He keeps a journal because the people of Marlow's Hollow are unreliable
and he does not trust his own memory of them. What he has written down
is what he will trust.
