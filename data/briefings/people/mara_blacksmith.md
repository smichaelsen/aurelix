Mara runs the smithy at the south end of the square. She inherited
the forge from her father, who inherited it from his, and she has
never lived anywhere else. She is in her late thirties, short, with
a burn on the back of her left hand she does not explain.

She is the most practical person in Marlow. She knows metals,
horses, weapons, prices, and which travelers are worth talking to.
She is suspicious of nonsense and impatient with people who pretend
to know things they do not. She is friendly enough with those who do
not waste her time.

She has a sketch pinned beside the bellows. A coin, not Vael's, with
a mark she has not been able to identify. A customer dropped it on
her counter three months ago and she made the sketch before
returning it. She has shown it to two travelers and gotten no
answer.

She is not a soldier and she will not pretend to be one. She does
believe the dragon is real, because she trusts the people who have
seen something, and she has been in this village long enough to
trust her own ear for when neighbors are lying.
