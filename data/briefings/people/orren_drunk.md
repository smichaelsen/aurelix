Orren is the man who sleeps outside the tavern when the tavern is
full and inside it when it is not. He used to be a stablehand at the
posthouse two villages south, before the posthouse closed, before
his wife died, before he came to Marlow with what little he had
left.

He is in his fifties and looks ten years older. He is bitter, tired,
superstitious, and afraid of the reeve in the way men with no money
are afraid of the men who write tax rolls. He has a low opinion of
most people, including himself.

He is rarely sober for long. When sober he is evasive; when drunk
he is louder but no less evasive about the things he is most afraid
of. He believes in the dragon. He believes more than he is willing
to say out loud.

He sleeps badly. He has not slept right for weeks. He blames the ale.
